FILE 09 / SERVICES / GAME TRAILER EDITING & SOUND DESIGN

Trailers That Make Games Look & Sound AAA

No vague quotes. No endless loops. Transparent, deliverable-driven production built to get indie games noticed and funded.

Service Overview

Built for indie devs on their 1st or 2nd game who need a punchy, atmospheric hook — Steam page, Kickstarter, or social drops — that makes their game look and sound like a AAA release. Every package separates asset delivery requirements (what you provide) from creative polish (what I own), so you’re never on the hook for things outside my control.

TIER 01 / STEAM & SOCIAL TEASER

The Steam / Social Teaser

15 – 30 Seconds

Quick social media drops, Twitter/X marketing hooks, or short-form Steam store loops.

  • 1x 30-second high-impact teaser trailer (1080p or 4K master file, optimized for Steam/YouTube)
  • Cut to rhythm with custom, proprietary sound design (Foley, UI hits, ambient transitions)
  • Custom dramatic musical sting or short dynamic loop (sourced/adapted from your library or custom composed)
  • 1 round of revisions

$350 – $500

Turnaround: 3–5 business days from asset delivery

TIER 02 / THE FLAGSHIP OFFER

The Core Gameplay Trailer

45 – 60 Seconds

Main Steam page launch trailers, Kickstarter campaigns, festival submissions.

  • 1x 60-second narrative-driven gameplay trailer
  • Pacing, shot selection, title/text card integration, and hook structuring
  • Full Audio Suite: complete sound design pass (impacts, weapon/mechanic sounds, UI, atmospheric beds, environmental audio layer)
  • Custom Music Track: bespoke, punchy musical arrangement tailored to the trailer's emotional escalation
  • 2 rounds of structural/polish revisions

$750 – $1,100

The sweet spot: affordable for an indie team pooling $1k together, high enough to compensate for a week of deep editing and custom scoring. Turnaround: 7–10 business days from asset delivery

TIER 03 / CINEMATIC SHOWCASE

The Cinematic & Gameplay Showcase

90 – 120 Seconds

Major publisher pitch drops, major festival showcases (Future Games Show, IGN, etc.), Kickstarter launch.

  • Up to 2 minutes of polished cinematic and gameplay intercut
  • Advanced narrative framing and psychological pacing (leveraging a horror/tension background)
  • Complete spatial sound design, dialogue cleanup/processing (if VO is included), custom multi-part dynamic music track
  • Project source file delivery option + 2 alternate aspect ratios (e.g., 9:16 vertical for TikTok/Shorts)
  • 3 rounds of revisions

$1,400 – $1,800

Turnaround: 10–14 business days

Asset Delivery & Terms

Asset Delivery

Required, so the build never stalls:

  • Raw gameplay footage delivered via Google Drive/Mega in minimum 1080p/60fps (log files/uncompressed preferred)
  • Footage organization help available as an add-on (+ $150)

Scope & Revisions

Protection on both sides:

  • Creative polish is included — clean, uncompressed captures are yours to provide
  • Additional revision rounds billed at $75/hour
  • Clear milestones, clean project files, zero guesswork

All services operate on a 50% deposit / 50% upon final master sign-off model.

Process & Deliverables

How It Works

  1. Discover call to understand your game's unique selling points
  2. Create trailer concept and storyboard
  3. Select and edit gameplay footage for maximum impact
  4. Create custom sound design and music
  5. Edit and refine within your included revision rounds
  6. Final delivery in multiple formats and resolutions

What You Get

  • High-quality video file (1080p, H.264; 4K on request)
  • Social media cutdowns (square, vertical versions)
  • Full mix and stems for audio flexibility
  • Guidance on release timing and platform specs
  • All materials ready for immediate distribution
