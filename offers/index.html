Discovery Consultation

Overview

A single-pass audit of an existing build designed to locate structural pacing bugs, tone disconnects, and audio/visual friction points before your players do.

Deliverables

  • Full build playthrough & notes
  • Tone alignment against reference titles
  • Pacing weak-point report
  • Written path-forward document

Pricing & Structure

  • Flat fee starting at $500 – $800 (Completed within 3 to 5 business days).

TIER II

Production Retainer

Overview

Ongoing monthly remote support that plugs directly into your pipeline to keep your build moving, your audio implemented, and your milestones on track.

Deliverables

  • Audio implementation support
  • Recurring level & pacing analysis
  • Trailer / cinematic construction
  • Monthly written status log

Pricing & Structure

  • Monthly retainer starting at $1,500 – $2,500/month (Hybrid options available featuring reduced base retainers paired with a modest 2% to 5% back-end royalty/revenue share for qualified indie teams).
